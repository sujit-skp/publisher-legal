Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Publisher is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

1. What Publisher stores, and where

Everything stays on the computer where you run Publisher:

  • Your library: the video files, thumbnails and text (titles, descriptions, captions) in the folders you choose. Publisher reads them; it does not copy them anywhere except to the platform you post to.
  • Sign-in keys: when you connect YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, the platform gives Publisher an access token. Publisher encrypts it and stores it in its data folder on your computer, locked to your Windows user account.
  • Account details: the name, username, profile picture and follower count of each connected account, so you can see which account you are posting to.
  • Upload history: what you posted, where and when, so you can follow progress.

2. What Publisher sends, and to whom

  • To the platform you choose: when you click to post or save a draft, Publisher sends that video, its caption and cover to that platform (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram/Facebook or TikTok) using its official API. Nothing is sent to any platform you have not connected or did not pick.
  • Temporary links: some platforms (for example Instagram) only accept a video or cover as a web link. For those, Publisher makes a temporary, unguessable link through Cloudflare that serves only that file, and closes it as soon as the platform has downloaded it.
  • Publisher does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or any other third-party service, and does not sell or share data.

3. Data from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook

Publisher asks only for the permissions it needs to show which account is connected and to publish the videos you choose (for TikTok: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). It does not read your private messages, contacts, other people’s content or analytics, and uses the data only to provide these features to you.

4. Keeping or removing your data

  • Disconnect an account in Publisher (Connections page) to delete its sign-in key from your computer. You can also remove Publisher’s access in each platform’s settings.
  • Delete Publisher’s data folder to remove everything it stored. Your videos and text files stay where they are.

5. Children

Publisher is a tool for creators and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

7. Contact
