Publisher

A desktop app for video creators. It runs on the creator’s own computer, keeps their library of finished videos and metadata, and publishes those videos to the creator’s own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts through each platform’s official API, only when the creator asks it to.

What it does

  • Lists the videos in folders the creator chooses, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails.
  • Connects to the creator’s accounts with the platforms’ own sign-in pages (OAuth). Publisher never sees passwords.
  • Uploads a video, with its caption and cover, to the account the creator picks, or saves it as a draft, when the creator clicks the button.
